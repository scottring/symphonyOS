The printable guide

Four sheets. None is a prerequisite for another.

Every sheet carries the same five bands in the same order, so the second sheet you pick up is already familiar. Bands 2–5 are each skippable and say so on the paper.

This is

The period and the year, in digits, written by hand. The one field that cannot be guessed from the rest of the page and must never be invented.

What happened

Three lines: done, carried, let go. No scoring and no ratings — the look-back exists to make the carry-forward decision deliberate.

What is already committed

Fixed dates, things that repeat anyway, and one capacity line in your own units. Before choosing anything new, not after.

What matters here

“By the end of this period, what do you want to be true?” Three numbered lines, two on the week sheet. The lines run out, which is the point.

What that means

Four printed areas — next actions, happens repeatedly, fixed date and time, not yet decided. Which area you write in is what classifies the line, so nothing has to be worded a particular way. Owner and date only when you know them.
