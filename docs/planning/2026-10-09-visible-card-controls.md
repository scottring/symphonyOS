# Visible card controls and distinct navigation

Every map card exposes Edit, Remove, and labeled Actions (shared triage). Parent cards also expose adding the next horizon's item. Column headers always expose independent creation, including focused branches; controls are outside the scrolled card list. Completed cards stay hidden by default with an explicit toggle.

Remove confirms period-only removal, or archives a yearly intention. Existing child data is retained. Permanent deletion remains in Actions with its separate confirmation. Failed writes keep the item visible and report failure.

Desktop and phone navigation now offer Today, Week, Plan. Existing Year/Season/Month URLs and their dates remain supported; Plan highlights for all three. Horizon controls inside the map are labeled Focus horizon.

Validated production build, 31 focused tests, desktop and 390px phone screenshots, direct editing dialog, existing signed-in card data. Regression coverage includes creation while focused, child creation context, failed removal, existing triage and connections. Screenshot: /private/tmp/symphony-visible-card-controls.png.
