lost) · `onboarding` (nothing is wrong; something needs teaching or a path).

mental model) · `medium` (friction, recoverable) · `low` (polish).

**Retest:** `open` · `fixed` · `retested-pass` · `retested-fail` · `wontfix` · `parked`.
