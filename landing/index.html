The coaching flywheel

It gets smarter
every week.

Your feedback drives a continuous improvement cycle. The coaching layer isn't static — it learns from your reality and adapts.

1

Live your day

Coaching blocks guide you through time-specific moments. After each block, tap a quick reaction: nailed it, okay, or tough.

2

Feedback accumulates

Reactions, tags, and notes flow into a coaching observations table. The AI remembers what worked and what didn't.

3

Weekly review

On Sunday, review the week's patterns. The AI reads your observations, published household rules, and research to generate next week's playbook.

4

Better next week

New coaching blocks reflect what you learned. Difficult transitions get more support. Smooth routines stay lean. The cycle continues.

Daily feedback → Coaching observations → AI-generated playbook → Better coaching → Repeat
