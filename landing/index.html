Built different

Three things we refuse to build.

No household manager

Most family apps quietly assume one person holds the plan and distributes it. Symphony is built for two adults with equal responsibility — nothing routes through a person, and there are no scoreboards keeping count.

No fake autopilot

We won't pretend software should decide your family's priorities. The clerical work — reading, retyping, reminding — goes to zero. The Sunday sit-down stays, and finally has everything it needs in one place.

No feature museum

Not another everything-app for families. One promise — Symphony knows what's going on in your household — and every screen exists to make that true.
